Data Sorter

Preview documents and images, assign categories and organise files into folders on your device.

Start the web app Download offline ZIP

Runs locally in Chrome or Edge. Your browser reads the files you select and writes results to the folders you choose.

How to sort files

  1. Choose your folders

    Pick the source and destination folders, then add the categories you need.

  2. Review and sort

    Inspect each file and assign a category. Choose whether to copy or move it.

  3. Save your progress

    Export a CSV of your decisions. Import it later to resume the session.

On first use, the browser stores about @@CACHE_MB@@ MB for offline use. The offline ZIP is @@ZIP_MB@@ MB. Extract it and open Data Sorter.html in Chrome or Edge.

Supported previews

JPEG, PNG, DICOM, PDF, DOCX, TXT and CSV. Use the zoom and navigation controls while reviewing, or filter the file list to undecided items.
